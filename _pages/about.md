I am Junteng Liu, a First-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Research Interests

My interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
